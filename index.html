    Privacy, backup & install

    SnapData stores these teacher-tool entries in this browser on this device. They are not uploaded to a server. Anyone who can use this browser profile may be able to access them, so avoid storing sensitive student information on shared devices.

    Offline app shell can open without internet after caching, but OCR libraries, AI summaries and some exports may need a connection. Do not include sensitive records in a backup unless you can store it securely.

    No accounts. No ads. Photos stay in your browser for OCR and editing. Summary text is sent to Gemini only when you request a summary.
